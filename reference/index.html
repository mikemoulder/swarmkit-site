SDK reference

The public TypeScript interface for procurement, replay, signing, and settlement.

Coordinator and allocation

ExportUse
createRoundConfigure buyers, sellers, one resource, one asset, three deadlines, and an optional clock and mechanism.
commitBidHash the round ID, seller ID, capacity, price, and a 32-byte salt. Generate a fresh cryptographic salt for live bids.
lowestPriceChoose the cheapest seller that can cover the entire pool within all buyer caps. Account ID breaks ties.
cheapestCapacityServe tight budgets first, drawing from lower-priced sellers. All demand must be filled. Charges use each seller’s bid.
simulateProcurementRun buyer and seller strategies with a seed. Inspect stage snapshots, the transcript and synthetic payments.

A custom ProcurementMechanism can select an eligible winner or supply split lines. Pin its implementation to a versioned ID when replaying a round.

Replay and storage

round.transcript() returns accepted events, the round specification, and its observation time. replayRound recomputes a snapshot. resumeRound reopens a round with a clock at or after that observation time.

Use openStoredRound and submitStoredRoundEvent with a RoundStore. Its compare-and-set operation must be atomic. A transcript proves consistency with its own recorded inputs. Use trusted storage and authenticate participants at your service boundary.

Settlement and NEAR

ExportUse
createSettlementPlanDerive payment IDs and amounts from a closed snapshot. Split plans use one ID per buyer–seller pair.
executeSettlementAuthorize, journal signed bytes, inspect the stored hash, and send or reconcile the same transaction.
executeWithSpendingPoliciesCheck buyer Ed25519 approvals against the plan’s asset, sellers, cumulative amounts, scope and expiry.
planNearSubaccountsPrepare create, fund, and add-key actions for direct children. The application supplies the parent signer.
nearTestnetTransportSettle native testnet NEAR with an application-supplied signer and check the finalized transfer.
nearIntentsTransportSettle supported NEP-141 Verifier balances with a buyer NEP-413 signer and relayer signer. Preflight and reconcile finalized execution.
auditSettlementReplay a transcript, check receipt IDs and hashes, and compare claimed and observed outcomes.

Settlement transfers are independent. A round can settle partially. A confirmed transfer requires a separate seller-authorized payment to refund. Keep journal entries private because they contain signed transaction bytes.
